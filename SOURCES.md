# Research sources and metric classifications

## Kolhapur District Environment Plan (2021)

Prepared by the Environment Department, Government of Maharashtra and Maharashtra Pollution Control Board. The plan reports 11.18 tonnes/day of plastic waste generation across **Kolhapur district**, including 6.08 tonnes/day attributed to the **Kolhapur Corporation** area. It also reports eight plastic recyclers in the district. The application uses the two generation figures only with their respective geographies and period. These are officially reported historical values, not current measurements.

[Open the official MPCB PDF](https://mpcb.gov.in/sites/default/files/environmentactionplan/Kolhapur23062021.pdf)

## MPCB Annual Report 2022–23, Table 5.63

MPCB reports 8,270 tonnes generated, 8,284 tonnes collected and 8,032 tonnes channelled for recycling for the **Kolhapur MPCB region**, comprising **44 urban local bodies**, for FY 2022–23. This is regional coverage, not Kolhapur city-only data. The report's collected figure exceeds generation; this discrepancy is retained as reported and not reconciled here.

[Open the official MPCB Annual Report PDF](https://mpcb.gov.in/sites/default/files/Establishment%20of%20MPCB/Seniority%20list/2014/MPCB%20English%20AR%202022-2023-06.06.2024-organized_0.pdf)

## Plastic Waste Management and EPR

CPCB hosts the Plastic Waste Management Rules and amendments, including the 2022 EPR guidelines and later updates. Applicable requirements can change; verify current rules and processor registration before relying on a compliance workflow. Collection on its own is not evidence of eligible processing or an EPR certificate.

- [CPCB Plastic Waste Management portal](https://pwm.cpcb.gov.in/)
- [CPCB Centralised EPR portal](https://eprplastic.cpcb.gov.in/)
- [MoEFCC / PIB explainer on 2022 EPR guidelines](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1898892&lang=2&reg=48)

No current city-only collection, recycling output, plant capacity, local price, named buyer agreement or payment data has been verified for this MVP. Those values are marked unavailable or simulated.
